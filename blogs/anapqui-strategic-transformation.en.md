## From Raw Material Export to Premium Consumer Brand: ANAPQUI's Strategic Transformation

Sitting on a "golden" reserve of organic raw materials — yet struggling with razor-thin margins while a multi-million-dollar factory runs at just 10% capacity. This was the complex supply chain and brand positioning puzzle that one of South America's largest agricultural cooperatives faced before turning to HLG Mooren Consulting.

**Project Background: The Giant's Paradox**
ANAPQUI is a cooperative of more than 1,500 farmers in Bolivia, holding exclusive cultivation rights over the world's largest certified organic Royal Quinoa growing region. Despite owning two ISO 22000-certified processing plants, the organisation was trapped in a "bulk raw material" model.

When we were brought in, ANAPQUI relied overwhelmingly on exporting 25 kg bulk bags of Quinoa grain — making them a pure price taker in a volatile global commodity market. Their domestic B2C product line showed real potential but lacked a sharp marketing strategy, competitive packaging, and the distribution network needed to reach modern retail channels. On top of that, the loose alignment of member farmers posed a direct threat to the stability and quality of the input supply chain.

**Our Approach: Value Chain Surgery**
Rather than delivering generic high-level advice, Hugo Mooren went directly into the field and conducted a full diagnostic of the business's operational health. We applied structured methodologies — SWOT Matrix, BCG Matrix, and Value Chain Analysis — to rigorously assess every link: from sourcing and production capacity at the Challapata and El Alto plants, through to pricing strategy and route-to-market. The ultimate goal: shift from volume to value, and from commodity to high-value-added products.

**Strategic Solutions Implemented**
Based on the on-the-ground diagnostic, HLG Mooren Consulting designed a comprehensive transformation roadmap built around our core competencies:

- **Marketing Strategy Repositioning:** We defined two distinct battlegrounds. For the domestic B2C market, the focus shifted to urban middle-class consumers through a messaging platform of "Safe, Nutritious & National Pride". High-potential product lines — cereals, pasta, and gluten-free biscuits — were repositioned to capture the rising healthy-eating trend.
- **Premium Export Strategy:** Instead of commodity bulk sales, we built a rigorous Distributor Selection Checklist to identify strategic partners in key European markets (Germany, France, Spain). The brand positioning was upgraded from "bulk Quinoa" to "Certified Organic Royal Quinoa" — targeting premium supermarket chains and Private Label partners.
- **Operations Restructuring & KPI Framework:** To address the loose management culture, we introduced a clear visual KPI Dashboard for the plant floor: tracking production volumes, cost per unit, on-time delivery rates, and First Time Right ratios.

![Factory operations](../assets/media/blog-img/anapqui-strategic-transformation/machine.png)

- **HR & Supply Chain Strategy:** A new communication protocol was established — using digital group channels and regional representatives to maintain direct engagement with all 1,500 member farmers — securing their loyalty and guaranteeing a stable, high-quality Quinoa supply.

**Value Delivered**
With the strategy redesigned by HLG Mooren Consulting, ANAPQUI now has a clear strategic roadmap to reverse their situation. The new strategic framework doesn't just help them unlock the dormant capacity of their multi-million-dollar production lines — it also creates a significant competitive moat by elevating brand equity. They are now positioned not merely as growers, but as owners of an organic food value chain with real global ambition.
