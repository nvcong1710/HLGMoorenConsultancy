# From a 50% Output Decline to a 350,000kg Roadmap: The 5-Year Restructuring Plan for Aprocam Cacao Cooperative

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/aprocam-restructuring-plan/stock.png)

When sales volume drops by 50% in a single year, it is no longer a market fluctuation—it is a structural crisis. Aprocam, a cacao cooperative in Bagua, Peru, faced three major challenges: near-total dependence on a single buyer, loss of farmers due to weak cash flow, and a severe production decline.

Yet the global cacao market was at a turning point: record-high prices and rising demand for high-quality cacao, especially in Europe. The real question was not “Is there an opportunity?” but **how to capture it through a structured, long-term strategy.**

## Project Background

- **Country:** Peru  
- **Region:** Bagua – Amazonas  
- **Model:** Organic cacao cooperative with centralized processing  
- **Products:** Organic & conventional cacao  
- **Main issue:**  
  - 50% decline in sales volume in the most recent year  
  - 80% of revenue dependent on one Italian chocolate producer  
  - Weak cash flow → loss of farmers  
  - High financial debt  
  - Weak commercial system  
  - No clear 5-year strategy  

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/aprocam-restructuring-plan/farm.png)

However, Aprocam had solid foundations:

- Organic certification  
- Fair Trade certification  
- Own infrastructure  
- Centralized fermentation and drying processes  
- Strong relationship with main customer  

The issue was not lack of potential—but lack of strategic structure.

## HLG Mooren Consulting’s Approach

We structured the advisory around four strategic pillars:

### 1️⃣ Strategic Repositioning: “Value over Volume”

Market analysis revealed:

- The cacao market is shifting toward premium & specialty segments  
- Prices are high, but production risks remain significant  
- Undifferentiated products risk being excluded from the value chain  

Strategic conclusion:  
👉 Focus on **premium cacao with clear traceability and consistent quality.**

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/aprocam-restructuring-plan/room.png)

### 2️⃣ Standardizing Centralized Processing

Risk of pod opening at farm level:

- Uncontrolled fermentation  
- Mold and acidification risks  
- Reduced commercial value  

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/aprocam-restructuring-plan/product.png)

Solution:

- Transport whole pods to the central facility  
- Centralized fermentation – drying – cleaning  
- Establish control over time, temperature, and oxygen  
- Four production KPI checkpoints:
  1. Kg harvested  
  2. Kg fermented  
  3. Kg dried  
  4. Kg cleaned  

Shift mindset from “collecting volume” to “controlling value.”

### 3️⃣ Customer Diversification & Concentration Risk Reduction

Current situation:

- 80% sold to one Italian buyer  
- 20% to one Belgian buyer  

5-year strategic targets:

- No single customer exceeding 60%  
- Secure 5 new customers by Year 2  
- Form alliances with other cooperatives (Year 3)  

This is a **commercial risk management strategy**, not just sales expansion.

### 4️⃣ 5-Year Growth Roadmap

The strategy was translated into annual milestones:

**Year 1**
- Recover farmers who left  
- Increase production to 175,000 kg  
- Maximize existing capacity  

**Year 2**
- Obtain HACCP certification  
- Standardize website & communications  
- Reduce customer dependency  
- Target sales: 200,000 kg  

**Year 3**
- Form alliances with other cooperatives  
- Target sales: 250,000 kg  

**Year 4**
- Optimize logistics (20ft → 40ft containers)  
- Reduce laboratory costs  
- Repay financial debt  
- Break-even at 300,000 kg  

**Year 5**
- Reach 350,000 kg  
- Invest in new equipment  
- Scale operations  

This is not a “dream growth plan,” but a prioritized and structured roadmap.

## Value Delivered

The strategy aims to:

- Shift from dependency to diversification  
- Upgrade from volume supplier to premium supplier  
- Increase value through traceability and quality consistency  
- Improve cash flow and retain farmers  
- Restore financial health within four years  

Most importantly, Aprocam now has a clear annual action plan—rather than reacting passively to market fluctuations.
