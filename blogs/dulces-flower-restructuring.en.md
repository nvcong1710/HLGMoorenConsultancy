# From a Local Family Business to a Nationwide Growth Strategy: The Restructuring Journey of Dulces Flower

In the food industry—especially in bakery ingredients—growth does not come solely from “good taste.” It comes from the right strategy, the right organization, and disciplined quality control.

Dulces Flower is a family business with over 30 years of experience in Medellín. But as the market evolved—modern supermarkets expanded, bakery chains scaled rapidly, and competition became increasingly professional—the old operating model was no longer sufficient to secure future positioning.

A comprehensive restructuring strategy became necessary.

---

## Project Background

- **Country:** Colombia
- **Industry:** Bakery ingredients – Arequipe & related products
- **Scale:** 85 employees, family-owned, operating since 1977
- **Core Market:** Medellín & Antioquia region

### Initial Challenges

A SWOT analysis revealed:

**Strengths**

- Strong product flavor profile
- Clean production facilities
- Solid customer relationships in Medellín
- Financial independence
- Strong family cohesion

**Weaknesses**

- No clear marketing strategy
- No presence in major cities such as Bogotá
- No quality certifications (HACCP / ISO)
- Complaint management not standardized
- Limited product diversification
- Unclear roles and responsibilities
- Distribution not nationwide

Meanwhile, competitors had:

- Automated production systems
- Longer shelf life
- Quality certifications
- Large sales teams
- Strong supermarket coverage

Dulces Flower was strong locally—but lacked the structural foundation to compete nationally.

---

## HLG Mooren Consulting’s Approach

The project was implemented in three strategic phases:

### 1️⃣ Deep Market & SWOT Analysis

- Assessed regional market share
- Benchmarked competitive capabilities against national players
- Analyzed distribution chain structures and distributor models

### 2️⃣ Development of a 5-Year Growth Strategy

- Reorganized the company into a marketing-driven structure
- Redesigned the sales system & distributor policy
- Built a roadmap for quality certification
- Optimized production and logistics

### 3️⃣ Department-Level Implementation

Across management, production, sales, complaint handling, and ERP integration.

Core expertise highlighted in this project:  
**Marketing Strategy + Distribution Strategy + Food Safety & Process Optimization**

---

## Key Solutions Implemented

### 1️⃣ Organizational Restructuring Toward Professionalization

- Defined six core divisions: Management – Sales – Production – Admin/Finance – Purchasing – HR
- Implemented regional Sales Management structure (Region 1–2–3)
- Standardized job descriptions & KPI systems
- Established budgeting and monthly/quarterly/annual reporting

---

### 2️⃣ Structured Distributor Policy

Instead of scattered sales through multiple small distributors:

- Selected top-tier distributors in major cities
- Defined clear selection criteria: Partnership – Long-term commitment – Openness – Growth orientation
- Applied a structured **Push & Pull strategy**
- Developed a professional distributor evaluation checklist

Objective: Expand nationally while maintaining brand control.

---

### 3️⃣ Marketing & Product Development Strategy

- Standardized packaging identity: one logo, one color scheme, one unified brand presence

![alt text](../assets/media/blog-img/dulces-flower-restructuring/packaging.png)

- Created Dulces Flower Group (holding structure)
- Developed new segments:
  - Consumer packaging for bakery use
  - Arequipe for the coffee segment
  - 10–15g portion packs for sampling

- Applied differentiated pricing strategies by segment: industry, bakery chains, foodservice

Dulces Flower began thinking not only as a manufacturer—but as a brand.

---

### 4️⃣ Production & Food Safety Upgrade

- Recommended implementation of HACCP / ISO 22000
- Standardized complaint handling system
- Optimized ERP utilization
- Improved cooking process and temperature control
- Proposed shift from sugar bags to bulk tank transport:
  - Reduced labor
  - Lowered costs
  - Improved consistency
  - Enabled automated dosing systems

Transitioned from batch-based to system-based production.

![alt text](../assets/media/blog-img/dulces-flower-restructuring/silo.png)

---

### 5️⃣ Structured Change Management

- Clear internal communication
- Staff training programs
- Appointment of change agents
- Measurement of adoption rates
- Reward systems for successful implementation

Because strategy only succeeds when people truly change.

---

## Value Delivered

The restructuring aimed to:

- Build the foundation for expansion into major cities
- Strengthen competitiveness against automated national players
- Increase brand credibility within supermarket systems
- Improve product consistency
- Reduce long-term production costs
- Prepare for international certifications

From a company strong in “taste,” Dulces Flower now has a roadmap to become strong in “system.”
