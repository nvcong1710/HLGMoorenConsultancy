I’ve just completed my second PUM assignment in Peru, and I’m grateful for the experience and impact it brought.

In the Amazon region of Mendoza, I had the opportunity to contribute to the strategic development of the Alta Montaña coffee cooperative, in close collaboration with DEVIDA, a Peruvian governmental organization supporting farmers in their transition from illegal crops to coffee and cacao farming.

Together, we worked on strengthening a long-term strategy for sustainable development, balancing economic growth with a positive impact on the ecosystem and a strong sense of social responsibility.

What made this project especially meaningful was the focus on the people behind the coffee and cacao:
the smallholder farmers and their families, whose resilience and future depend on access to fair markets, sustainable practices, and viable alternatives.

I’m inspired by the commitment and ambition of everyone involved, and proud to have contributed—however modestly—to a more sustainable, inclusive, and responsible coffee and cacao value chain.

Thank you to all partners and stakeholders for the trust and collaboration. 🌱☕🍫
Looking forward to seeing Alta Montaña continue to grow its impact.
