# From 1% Net Profit to a 10% EBIT Roadmap: The Comprehensive Restructuring Journey of Garner Wafers

When a company achieves double-digit revenue growth but net profit remains around 1%, the issue is no longer the market—it is the operating system. Garner Wafers, a producer of waffles and ice cream cones in South Africa, faced exactly this paradox: 12% growth, yet margins too thin to sustain long-term development.

The challenge was not simply “cut costs,” but to restructure production, logistics, quality, and marketing strategy in an integrated way.

---

## Project Background

- **Country:** South Africa  
- **Category:** Ice cream cones, waffles, coatings – B2B & retail  
- **Scale:** 60 FTE  
- **Revenue:** ~39 million ZAR  
- **Net Profit:** ~1.2%  
- **Customers:** Supermarket chains, restaurants, ice cream shops, distributors  

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/garner-wafers-restructuring/retail-pr.png)

### Key Challenges

- 80% of revenue dependent on four major customers  
- Produce-to-order model causing instability  
- OTIF (On Time In Full) at ~65%  
- ERP not optimized for manufacturing  
- HACCP certified, but no higher-level certifications such as BRC  
- Low productivity, high night-shift costs  
- No clear KPIs across departments  

Target defined: **10% EBIT**.

## Strategic Solutions Implemented

### 🔹 1. Shift from Produce-to-Order to Produce-to-Stock

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/garner-wafers-restructuring/structure.png)

- Stabilized production planning  
- Increased batch sizes → reduced cost per unit  
- Improved OTIF performance  
- Optimized capacity utilization  

Expected outcome: lower labor costs, reduced overtime, improved service levels.

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/garner-wafers-restructuring/ice-cream.png)

---

### 🔹 2. ERP Optimization & KPI Management

- Completed production data and product specifications  
- Established line-level KPIs: output/hour, waste %, capacity utilization  
- Weekly variance meetings (actual vs. budget)  
- SKU-level profitability analysis  

ERP was repositioned from an accounting system to a **strategic management tool**.

---

### 🔹 3. Quality & Traceability Upgrade

- Standardized Best Before Date (BBD) & traceability systems  
- Implemented product tolerance controls (traffic-light system)  
- Improved separation of high-care and low-care areas  
- Enforced 100% compliance in documentation  

This formed the foundation for upgrading toward BRC or ISO 22000 certification.

---

### 🔹 4. Logistics & OTIF Optimization

- Drop-cost per stop analysis  
- Palletization optimization  
- Benchmarking outsourcing for inefficient routes  
- Defined minimum order quantities  

Objective: increase OTIF from 65% to 95%.

---

### 🔹 5. Marketing & Pricing Strategy Redesign

Five strategic positioning options were defined:

- Niche player  
- Low-cost producer (cones)  
- Innovator (NPD pipeline)  
- Regional market-share focus  
- Market leader in cones  

Pricing differentiation applied:

- Skimming for new product development  
- Value-for-money for premium segment  
- Cost-leader pricing for mainstream  

Branding structured separately:

- B2B outer-box branding  
- B2C consumer pull strategy  

Marketing was repositioned from cost center to margin lever.

---

## Value Delivered

The restructuring roadmap was designed to:

- Increase EBIT from 1% to 10%  
- Reduce night-shift labor costs  
- Improve OTIF to 95%  
- Strengthen negotiation power with major customers  
- Prepare for higher-level quality certifications  
- Shift from reactive operations to data-driven proactive management  

Most importantly, Garner Wafers now has a **clear, measurable, and realistic transformation roadmap**.
