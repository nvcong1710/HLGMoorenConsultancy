# From 95% Idle Capacity to a 2026–2030 Growth Strategy: The Strategic Turning Point of Thanh Dong Mushroom

A farm with significant production capability—but operating at only 5% utilization.  
A market where demand exceeds supply—yet profitability remains unstable.

This was the paradox facing Thanh Dong Company in Buon Ma Thuot, Vietnam.

![alt text](../assets/media/blog-img/thanh-dong-strategy/farm.png)

The issue was not the product. Reishi (Linh Chi) and medicinal mushrooms are experiencing strong growth in Vietnam. The real challenge lay in **market strategy, channel structure, and profit management**.

---

## Project Background

- **Location:** Buon Ma Thuot, Dak Lak, Vietnam
- **Industry:** Medicinal mushrooms (Reishi, Turkey Tail, etc.)
- **Model:** Cultivation – Drying – Packaging – B2B & B2C
- **Core Issues:**
  - 95% underutilized farm capacity
  - 70% of revenue dependent on two B2B customers
  - No fully implemented ISO 22000 system
  - No dedicated sales & quality personnel
  - No profitability analysis per customer or product
  - Suboptimal factory conditions (humidity control, traceability gaps)

![alt text](../assets/media/blog-img/thanh-dong-strategy/mushroom.png)

### Market Reality

- Domestic medicinal mushroom demand exceeds supply (only ~33% fulfilled locally)
- Vietnam heavily imports from China
- Strong growth in Healthy Food and Supplements segments

The opportunity was clear—but required structured execution.

![alt text](../assets/media/blog-img/thanh-dong-strategy/meeting.png)

## HLG Mooren Consulting’s Strategic Approach

We structured the advisory around five pillars:

### 1️⃣ SWOT & Internal Capability Analysis

**Strengths**

- Strong cultivation knowledge
- Links with universities and government agencies
- Relatively standardized growing process
- Sustainable production (solar energy integration)

**Weaknesses**

- No SKU-level profitability clarity
- Incomplete food safety certification
- Significant overcapacity
- Customer concentration risk

---

### 2️⃣ Market Strategy Decision: B2B or B2C?

Three strategic options were evaluated:

- B2C focus (higher margin, slower scale)
- B2B focus (faster volume, lower margin)
- Combination strategy

![alt text](../assets/media/blog-img/thanh-dong-strategy/mr-lr.png)

### Recommended Strategy

👉 **B2B as the cash-flow engine**  
👉 **B2C as the brand and margin engine**

Core principle:  
**“Use B2B to fund B2C—not the other way around.”**

---

### 3️⃣ Application of Ansoff & BCG Models

We applied:

- **Ansoff Matrix** (market penetration & product development focus)
- **BCG Matrix** (portfolio prioritization)

Strategic classification:

- Traditional Reishi = Cash Cow
- B2C tea bags = Star
- Energy mushroom concept = Question Mark

The portfolio gained clarity and prioritization.

---

## Key Solutions Implemented

### 🔹 1. Filling Capacity Through Strategic B2B Expansion

- Appointment of dedicated B2B Key Account Manager
- Long-term contracts
- Competitive pricing strategy
- Faster break-even achievement

Idle capacity was repositioned as a growth lever.

---

### 🔹 2. Origin-Based Branding for Reishi

Brand narrative structured as:

Vietnam → Dak Lak → Buon Ma Thuot → Thanh Dong Farm

- Strong storytelling around cultivation region
- Dedicated B2C packaging development
- Positioning: **Healthy – Sustainable – Transparent**

Brand defined as trust—not just visual identity.

---

### 🔹 3. Quality System Standardization

- Roadmap toward HACCP / ISO 22000 / Organic certification
- Traceability system establishment
- Humidity control improvements
- Standardized drying & packaging processes

Objective: differentiate from low-cost imported mushrooms lacking quality control.

---

### 🔹 4. Distribution Strategy

**B2B**

- Direct contracts with large institutional buyers

**B2C**

- Selected distributors in major cities (Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Hai Phong, etc.)
- Distributor criteria: Partnership – Long-term orientation – Commitment – Growth
- Clear channel conflict avoidance between B2B and B2C

---

### 🔹 5. Controlled Capacity Expansion

- Planned 1.8x capacity increase
- Harvest-cycle-based production analysis
- Budget roadmap through 2030
- Separate B2B and B2C investment budgets

Growth shifted from intuition-based decisions to structured financial planning.

---

## Value Delivered

The 2026–2030 strategy was designed to:

- Convert 95% idle capacity into growth momentum
- Create stable B2B cash flow
- Build a strong domestic Reishi brand
- Reduce dependency on imports
- Prepare systems for export readiness
- Increase value-added positioning rather than competing on price

Thanh Dong is no longer just a mushroom farm—it is evolving into a strategically structured enterprise.
