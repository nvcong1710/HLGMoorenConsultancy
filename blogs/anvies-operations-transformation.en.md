## From a Local Processing Workshop to a Standardized System: The Transformation Roadmap of Anvies Agricultural Brand

![alt text](<../assets/media/blog-img/anvies-operations-transformation/Screenshot 2026-02-28 130123.png>)

Owning a high-quality product yet struggling with sales execution, lacking standardized management systems, and operating without internationally recognized food safety certifications required to enter demanding markets—this is a common pain point for many SMEs in the F&B industry. It was also the core challenge Anvies faced before partnering with HLG Mooren Consulting.

![alt text](<../assets/media/blog-img/anvies-operations-transformation/Screenshot 2026-02-28 130242.png>)

**Project Background: A Gem in the Rough**  
Headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City (Vietnam), Anvies is a passionate enterprise specializing in sustainable processing of vegetables and mushrooms using diverse technologies: freeze-drying, hot-air drying, and freezing. Their strongest advantage lies in superior product quality and tightly controlled raw material sourcing thanks to their proximity to cultivation areas (just a two-hour drive).

However, the internal operational landscape revealed critical weaknesses: no dedicated sales team, production processes not meeting international standards (no HACCP, BRC, or ISO certifications), and a complete absence of a structured marketing strategy. Financial data systems were opaque, with no ERP or KPI measurement framework. Most critically, their export activity carried substantial risk due to dependence on a single U.S. partner.

**Our Approach: “Build the Foundation Before Scaling”**  
Under the leadership of consultant Hugo Mooren, HLG Mooren Consulting did not rely on abstract theory. We conducted a comprehensive business diagnostic, clearly identifying internal constraints through SWOT analysis and assessing the product portfolio using the BCG matrix.

Our consulting philosophy is pragmatic: Anvies cannot sell at high value if its internal production “lungs” do not meet international standards.

![alt text](<../assets/media/blog-img/anvies-operations-transformation/Screenshot 2026-02-28 130023.png>)

**Strategic Solutions Implemented**  
Together with Anvies’ leadership team, we executed a comprehensive restructuring centered around key pillars:

- **Standardizing Production Processes (HACCP & Operations):**  
  Immediate identification and elimination of cross-contamination risks within the workshop (e.g., motorbikes inside production areas, wooden tools prone to mold, and raw material handling directly on the floor).  
  Simultaneously, we established a clear production KPI system to measure waste ratios, labor productivity, and the actual cost per drying batch.

- **Multi-Channel B2B Distribution Strategy:**  
  Developed a structured Distributor Checklist to penetrate the domestic HORECA segment (hotels, restaurants, industrial catering), creating stable cash flow as a foundation before expanding exports into new markets.

**Expected Results: Transform to Grow**  
With HLG Mooren Consulting’s in-depth advisory roadmap, Anvies has been placed on a professional growth platform. From an organically developed processing workshop, the company now possesses an operational framework aligned with stringent international food safety standards, supported by a commercially optimized product portfolio.

This strategy not only strengthens their domestic market position but also prepares them to meet rigorous due diligence requirements from future international distribution partners.
