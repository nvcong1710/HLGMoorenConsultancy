# From Handmade Ovens to a Structured HACCP System: The Comprehensive Upgrade of Ebeston Bakery in Ghana

Many bakery businesses across Africa share a common pattern: products sell well, cash flow is positive—but operational systems are not aligned with international standards.

Ebeston Bakery was no exception. As a young enterprise in Wa, Ghana, the company was already profitable, had loyal customers, and maintained low operating costs. However, once the ambition to become the regional market leader was defined, a critical question emerged:

How can sustainable growth be achieved when operational foundations and food safety systems are not yet standardized?

---

## Project Background

- **Country:** Ghana  
- **Industry:** Fresh bakery / bread production  
- **Scale:** 60 FTE, 1.5 million loaves per year  
- **Goal:** Become market leader in the WA area  

### Key Challenges

A SWOT analysis revealed:

### External Threats
- Unstable electricity supply  
- Shortage of skilled bakery staff  
- Weak infrastructure  
- Complex food regulations  
- Numerous small competitors  

### Internal Weaknesses
- No HACCP system  
- No standardized production procedures  
- No cleaning protocol  
- Handmade ovens without temperature control  
- Uncontrolled cross-contamination risks  
- No structured costing (COS)  
- No production reporting  
- No quality control system  

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/ebeston-bakery-haccp/building-2022.png)

The most critical issue:  
A handmade oven (~€400), without insulation, temperature control, or timer—leading to **irregular baking quality**.

Ebeston had a good product—but lacked system consistency.

## HLG Mooren Consulting’s Approach

The project was structured around four strategic pillars:

1️⃣ Establish Food Safety & HACCP foundations  
2️⃣ Standardize Production Planning & Reporting  
3️⃣ Optimize production capacity & equipment  
4️⃣ Build Marketing & Growth Strategy  

Core expertise highlighted:  
**HACCP Implementation + Production Optimization + Bakery Process Structuring**

---

## Key Solutions Implemented

### 1️⃣ HACCP Foundation & Hygiene Protocols

- Mandatory hand washing before entering production area  
- Removal of wood materials from production zones  
- Replacement of wooden tables with compliant materials  
- Implementation of structured cleaning procedures  
- Foreign body control system  
- Hygiene training for all employees  

Shifted from “habit-based baking” to “process-based baking.”

---

### 2️⃣ Production Planning Standardization

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/ebeston-bakery-haccp/transport.png)

- Daily/weekly/monthly planning vs. actual comparison  
- Implementation of structured production planning system  
- Introduction of production reporting  
- Bottleneck analysis of the 377-minute process (6.17 hours total cycle time)  

Measurement enabled waste reduction and output improvement.

---

### 3️⃣ Equipment Upgrade & Baking Quality Control

**Current situation:**
- No insulation  
- No temperature control  
- No timer  
- Only bottom heat  

**Recommendations:**
- Improve oven insulation  
- Introduce temperature control systems  
- Optimize proofing and baking times  
- Standardize baking capacity per tin size  
- Improve mixer & kneader safety systems  

Objective: Achieve consistency before expansion.

---

### 4️⃣ Delivery & Logistics Optimization

Current situation:
- 70 drops/stops per route  
- 3 tricycles, 2 vans  
- No structured route planning  

Solutions:
- Drop density analysis  
- Optimization of minutes per drop  
- Increase average drop size  
- Reduce cost per loaf  

Focus: Improve efficiency without expanding fleet size.

---

### 5️⃣ Packaging & Branding Strategy

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/ebeston-bakery-haccp/packaging.png)

Packaging was redefined as a marketing tool:

- Communication channel  
- Product protection  
- Shelf-life extension  
- Brand recognition driver  

Recommended a hybrid solution: pre-printed bags combined with flexible sticker systems to maintain professionalism while controlling costs.

---

### 6️⃣ HR & Training

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/ebeston-bakery-haccp/consumer.png)

- On-the-job training programs  
- Clear KPI definition  
- Performance-based incentives  
- Defined responsibilities per role  
- Strengthened commitment & discipline  

Because equipment alone cannot replace well-trained people.

---

## Value Delivered

The strategy was designed to:

- Standardize production according to normative standards  
- Increase product consistency  
- Prepare the foundation for HACCP certification  
- Increase production capacity without proportional cost increase  
- Optimize delivery and logistics costs  
- Build a reliable regional bakery brand  

From experience-based baking to a structured international-standard bakery system, Ebeston now has a clear roadmap for sustainable growth.
