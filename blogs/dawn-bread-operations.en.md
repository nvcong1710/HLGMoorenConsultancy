## Breaking Space Constraints: Supply Chain & Operations Optimization for FMCG Leader Dawn Bread

When a company grows rapidly, outdated infrastructure and legacy processes quickly become restrictive. How can a leading FMCG brand resolve space bottlenecks, optimize a large-scale supply chain, and increase capacity by 20%—without spending millions on factory expansion? That was the challenge HLG Mooren Consulting was brought in to solve.

**Project Background: The Tight-Fitting Shirt of a Market Leader**  
Dawn Bread is the dominant bread brand in Lahore, Pakistan—a densely populated market rapidly shifting toward modern retail consumption. While serving thousands of B2B customers and supermarkets daily, its central factory was struggling with multiple operational bottlenecks.

Production was fragmented across three floors, causing severe internal logistics congestion. Warehouse areas were overcrowded with finished goods and plastic crates due to inefficient circulation processes. Meanwhile, the sales force was trapped in a cycle of manual order-taking and cash collection, driving outbound logistics costs to alarming levels. The system required a structural overhaul—not a temporary fix.

## Strategic Solutions Implemented

### 1️⃣ Inbound & Storage Optimization

- Replaced manual unloading with standardized plastic pallet systems and reach trucks to improve space utilization and reduce labor intensity.  
- Installed indoor flour silos to fully automate dosing processes—reducing raw material losses and freeing significant warehouse space.

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/dawn-bread-operations/silo.png)

This shifted operations from manual handling to structured, scalable logistics.

---

### 2️⃣ Operational Turnaround & Capacity Expansion

Previously, the plant operated two 10-hour shifts (with overtime), creating inefficiencies due to repeated startup and oven heating cycles.

We redesigned operations into a 3-shift, 8-hour model running 24/7. This eliminated restart costs and directly increased capacity by **20%—without adding new production lines**.

Additionally:
- Integrated automated bread bagging systems  
- Reduced cross-contamination risks  
- Lowered manpower requirements  
- Improved overall production consistency  

The result was a structural productivity gain—not incremental improvement.

---

### 3️⃣ Sales Transformation & Digitalization

In a market where 90% of customers own smartphones, we introduced a B2B ordering application strategy.

This transformation:
- Freed the sales team from manual order recording  
- Reduced cash-handling inefficiencies  
- Converted sales representatives into strategic account managers focused on expanding new outlets and strengthening customer relationships  

We also aligned the roadmap toward ERP integration (SAP HANA) to unify company-wide data flows.

![alt text](../HLGMoorenConsultancy/assets/media/blog-img/dawn-bread-operations/marketing-strategy.png)

This marked a shift from operational firefighting to structured digital governance.
